Our Story

Rooted in Sialkot, Built for Pakistan

Eman Rice Mills was founded in 2014 by Ansar Mehmood Sandhu and Muhammad Azam Sandhu with a clear vision: to bring carefully milled, honest-quality rice from Sialkot's agricultural heartland to households, retailers, wholesalers, and businesses across Pakistan. From Village Langyawali, P.O Satrah, Tehsil Daska, the mill has grown through consistent work, reliable supply, and a reputation for keeping its word.

Today, under the operational leadership of General Manager Muhammad Farooq, every stage of procurement, cleaning, milling, grading, and dispatch is handled with care. We focus on the domestic Pakistan market only, giving our customers fresh supply, practical service, and a deeply local standard of trust.
